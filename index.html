Touchè Lifestyle Café, known to regulars as TLC, is a pub and café in Lerulaneng, Rabokala. It is built for long, easy afternoons and evenings with friends, family and a good plate of food.

Most guests spend between R100 and R400 per person, so it works for a quick bite or a full night out.
